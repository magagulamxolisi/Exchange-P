About the Programme

Built to challenge how you think

Meridian Exchange is a structured, peer-to-peer dialogue programme for young professionals aged 22–35. Once a month, a cohort of South African and Russian participants gather online to discuss everything from leadership and entrepreneurship to identity, politics, and culture.

The goal is not to reach agreement. It is to expand the frame — to help South African young professionals in particular develop a genuinely global perspective, shaped not by headlines but by direct human conversation.

  • Peer-to-peer: no lecturers, no one-directional learning — equals in dialogue.
  • Deliberately diverse topics: professional and social themes, mixed intentionally.
  • Fully virtual: no visa barriers, no travel costs, no gatekeeping.
  • Norm-challenging by design: sessions are structured to surface and question assumptions.

Why Join

Three reasons this matters

We are not building a networking event. We are building a long-term shift in how a generation of professionals see the world.

01

Think globally, genuinely

Most international exposure for South African professionals is limited to Western contexts. Meridian Exchange deliberately opens a different window — one that is equally valuable, and far less travelled.

02

Build a peer network that lasts

Six months of regular contact builds something fundamentally different from a one-off conference. Participants leave with real relationships — people they can call, collaborate with, and learn from for years.

03

Be challenged in ways that matter

Sessions are explicitly designed to surface and question assumptions — about leadership, success, identity, and how the world works. Comfortable conversations don't change how you think. These will.
